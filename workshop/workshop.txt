description=[h1]LX Split-Screen Skills Boost[/h1]
description=Skill boost for every local character in Project Zomboid Build 42 (B42) splitscreen / split screen / local couch co-op. Skip the grind when 2, 3 or 4 of you share one screen.
description=Two people on one couch should be building and fighting, not grinding the same early levels twice.
description=Split-screen is the mode that carries the most bugs in stable B42 - and the mods worth playing with, like Bandits and Project A-Life, are built for singleplayer and online multiplayer. Nobody is fixing couch co-op.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]A floor, not a handout[/b] - Levels are only ever [b]raised[/b]. A character who is already above the floor keeps everything they have - the mod never takes a level away.
description=[*][b]Two groups, both adjustable[/b] - Combat (Strength, Fitness, Aiming, Reloading) defaults to 10.
description=[*][b]Every local character[/b] - Players 1 through 4, each once, each tracked separately.
description=[*][b]Recipes come with the levels[/b] - It raises skills through the same path the game's own debug menu uses, which fires the level-up event - so the recipes those levels auto-unlock are actually learned, instead of you having the level…
description=[*][b]Top up later[/b] - Raise the floor in the options and existing characters catch up the next time they spawn. Lower it and nothing is taken away.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] Nothing. Vanilla B42.21 is enough.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
description=[b]Options:[/b] Options → MODS → [b]LX Split-Screen Skills Boost[/b]. Master switch, combat floor (0–10) and crafting floor (0–10).

description=[list]
description=[*][b]Un piso, no un regalo[/b] - Los niveles solo [b]suben[/b]. Un personaje que ya está por encima del piso conserva todo lo que tiene - el mod nunca saca un nivel.
description=[*][b]Dos grupos, los dos ajustables[/b] - Combate (Fuerza, Estado físico, Puntería, Recarga) viene en 10.
description=[*][b]Cada personaje local[/b] - Jugadores 1 a 4, cada uno una vez, cada uno registrado por separado.
description=[*][b]Las recetas vienen con los niveles[/b] - Sube las habilidades por el mismo camino que usa el menú de debug del juego, que dispara el evento de subida de nivel - así las recetas que esos niveles desbloquean solas se aprenden de verdad…
description=[*][b]Completar después[/b] - Subí el piso en las opciones y los personajes existentes se ponen al día la próxima vez que aparezcan. Bajálo y no se saca nada.
description=[/list]

description=[b]Opciones:[/b] Opciones → MODS → [b]LX Pantalla dividida: mejora de habilidades[/b]. Interruptor general, piso de combate (0–10) y piso de crafteo (0–10).
